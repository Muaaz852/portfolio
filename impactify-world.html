A crypto-donation platform that lets NGOs accept Bitcoin and Ethereum through an embeddable, compliance-ready checkout widget.

Impactify World

Book a call LinkedIn GitHub

Impactify World is a crypto-donation platform I built in three parts: an NGO portal where charities onboard, customize their donation page, and track gifts; an embeddable donation widget that lives on the NGO's own website; and a backend that handles payments through Coinify, compliance, emails, and real-time status updates.

Who it's for

  • NGOs and non-profits accepting cryptocurrency donations
  • Donors who prefer giving in Bitcoin, Ethereum, or other crypto
  • Platform admins who onboard and oversee organizations

The problem

Most NGOs can't build crypto infrastructure themselves — there's no way to accept Bitcoin or Ethereum on their own site, real worries about KYC and GDPR compliance, risky manual wallet handling, and no dashboard to see who donated, how much, and in which currency.

Key flows

  • NGO onboarding. Register, complete a guided setup, add organization details, verify the domains allowed to host the widget, design the donation page, and embed the generated code.
  • Donor checkout. Choose an amount and cryptocurrency with a live conversion estimate, enter details (or donate anonymously), accept GDPR, pass reCAPTCHA, then send crypto to a Coinify wallet address and watch live status through to a receipt.
  • Donation tracking. The portal lists every donation with status, filters by date, amount, or status, and exports to CSV, with email alerts when new donations arrive.
  • Compliance & trust. Domain verification, reCAPTCHA, GDPR consent, and KYC questions with document upload for donations over €10,000.

Value delivered

  • NGOs accept crypto on their own website with no technical skills
  • Compliance handled — GDPR, KYC, and domain security
  • Real-time donation tracking and CSV export
  • A clear, confidence-building checkout for donors
  • A multi-tenant platform with admin oversight of every organization

Built with

  • Vue.js
  • Quasar
  • NestJS
  • MySQL
  • Prisma
  • Coinify API
  • TypeScript
