A dual-portal renewable-energy platform where corporates trace their green power to its source assets and utilities allocate Guarantees of Origin.

Renuw

Book a call LinkedIn GitHub

Renuw is a dual-portal renewable-energy management platform I built. The corporate portal lets companies monitor consumption, trace their energy back to the exact wind and solar assets on a map, shape their portfolio, and generate audit-ready ESG reports; the utility and trader portal lets providers manage assets, customers, AI-powered Guarantee of Origin allocation, and billing. Both share one role-based sign-in with a guided onboarding wizard and an interactive walkthrough.

Who it's for

  • Corporate sustainability teams proving where their renewable energy comes from
  • Utilities and energy traders who own wind and solar assets and allocate Guarantees of Origin (GOs)
  • ESG and compliance officers responsible for Scope 2 reporting and audits

The problem

Companies committing to renewable energy face a transparency gap: they buy green power but cannot prove which wind farm or solar park supplied it, consumption and certificate data live in separate systems, and Scope 2 ESG reports mean manual spreadsheet work every quarter. Utilities, meanwhile, struggle to allocate certificates across many corporate customers, and regulators increasingly demand hourly time-matching rather than annual green labels.

Key flows

  • Corporate onboarding. A guided five-step wizard - account, portfolio preferences, utility provider, Power-of-Attorney authorization, and review - configures a new company in a single session.
  • Corporate dashboard. A time-matching score, key metrics like annual consumption and CO2 avoided, and a consumption-versus-green-supply graph show how green a company really is with data, not claims.
  • Certificate traceability. An interactive map traces energy back to the exact wind farms and solar parks, with Guarantee of Origin certificates and hour-by-hour matching for an audit-ready chain of custody.
  • ESG reporting. One-click Scope 2 reports with renewable percentage, time-matching score, market-based emissions, and source breakdown, plus a searchable report history.
  • Utility assets & customers. Operators manage their wind and solar fleet with health monitoring and maintenance scheduling, and run a CRM of corporate customers with consumption and matching scores.
  • AI-powered GO allocation. The core utility engine distributes Guarantees of Origin across assets and customers to maximize each client's time-matching score, with billing tied to actual allocation and usage.

Value delivered

  • Prove renewable claims with map-based traceability, not just annual labels
  • Real hourly time-matching scores and one-click, audit-ready ESG reports
  • Corporate control over portfolio mix, location, and matching preferences
  • Full renewable asset-fleet management with health monitoring
  • Intelligent GO certificate allocation across customers, with usage-based billing
  • A two-sided platform connecting energy producers with corporate buyers
