A live-commerce marketplace where sellers sell on camera and buyers shop, chat, and check out without leaving the stream.

ShoowMe

Book a call LinkedIn GitHub

ShoowMe is a live-commerce marketplace I built where sellers go live on camera, showcase products, and sell in real time. Buyers watch streams, chat with sellers, add items to cart, and place orders without leaving the live session — live streaming, an online shop, chat, and order management in one platform.

Who it's for

  • Sellers who want to sell products through live video
  • Buyers who enjoy interactive, social shopping
  • Platform admins who manage users, orders, and payouts

The problem

Traditional online stores are static, and creators on Instagram or TikTok can't easily turn live viewers into buyers. There was no dedicated place for live demos with instant purchasing, no real-time Q&A while watching a product, and fulfilment and payouts were disconnected from the live experience.

Key flows

  • Seller goes live. Set up a shop, add products, and start a stream that appears on the buyer home feed; demo products with featured items beside the video and take orders in real time.
  • Buyer shops live. Browse trending streams, join a live room, watch and chat, add to cart, check out, and track orders — and revisit any seller's shop profile outside of streams.
  • Order lifecycle. Cart to order to seller approval, status updates, waybill generation, and automatic tracking via a NinjaVan logistics webhook.
  • Earnings & payouts. Sellers request payouts from accumulated earnings and an admin approves or declines them.
  • Admin oversight. Manage users, monitor platform-wide orders, and approve payouts.

Value delivered

  • Sellers turn live audiences into paying customers
  • Everything in one place: stream, shop, orders, and earnings
  • Permanent shop profiles buyers can revisit
  • Real-time chat that builds trust and drives conversions
  • Admin-controlled payouts that keep the marketplace trustworthy

Built with

  • React
  • TypeScript
  • Express.js
  • ZegoCloud
  • Socket.IO
  • Vite
