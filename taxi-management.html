Taxi Management

Book a call LinkedIn GitHub

Taxi Management is an offline-first fleet system I built in three parts: a driver mobile app for starting shifts and logging rides and payments (working even with no signal), an admin web panel where managers see every shift, ride, driver, and car, and a backend that syncs mobile data to the cloud when drivers regain connectivity.

Who it's for

  • Taxi drivers logging shifts and rides from their phone
  • Fleet managers and dispatchers overseeing drivers, cars, and revenue
  • Owners who want accurate records of every trip

The problem

Many taxi operations still run on paper logbooks, radio calls, and memory — entries get lost, there's no signal in tunnels or basements so rides go unrecorded, managers stay blind until paperwork is handed in, mixed cash-and-card payments are hard to track, and there's no audit trail for disputes.

Key flows

  • Driver's day. Log in, start a shift with car and odometer, record each ride's pickup, destination, and payment (split across methods if needed), then end the shift — everything saves on the phone instantly.
  • Offline sync. Rides recorded with no connection are stored locally, then pushed to the server and reconciled the moment the signal returns.
  • Manager's view. A dashboard plus shifts, shift detail, and a cross-shift rides view with search and filters by driver, car, date, and shift type — alongside user and car management and activity logs.
  • Multi-payment rides. A single fare can be split across cash and card as separate payment lines that total correctly and show in the rides table.

Value delivered

  • Drivers log a ride in seconds and never lose data to bad signal
  • Managers get real-time fleet visibility as data syncs
  • Accurate revenue tracking per shift and per ride
  • Audit logs for disputes and accountability
  • Paper logbooks and manual tallying eliminated
