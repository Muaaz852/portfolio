A web-based accounting platform that unifies invoices, bills, customers, vendors, and bookkeeping in one dashboard.

BarqBooks

Book a call LinkedIn GitHub

BarqBooks is a web-based accounting platform I built to bring sales, purchases, and bookkeeping into a single dashboard. A business owner logs in, sees their latest invoices and bills immediately, and manages customers, products, vendors, invoices, bills, accounts, and transactions from one sidebar.

Who it's for

  • Small and medium business owners
  • Bookkeepers and finance teams

The problem

Before a tool like this, records were scattered — invoices in one folder, vendor bills in another, customer details in email. There was no single view of money coming in versus going out, totals and due dates were worked out by hand, and there was no structured chart of accounts or role-based access.

Key flows

  • Getting started. Sign up, land on the dashboard, and set up the basics — products, customers, vendors, and a chart of accounts.
  • Invoicing. Add a customer and products, then create an invoice with line items and discounts; subtotal and total are calculated automatically.
  • Bills. Add vendors and record bills with line items so payables sit right next to receivables.
  • Accounting. Set up the chart of accounts and review the transaction ledger to reconcile and audit.
  • Daily check-in. Filter invoices and bills by account, status, or date and drill into the details that need attention.

Value delivered

  • One platform instead of spreadsheets, email, and paper
  • Faster invoicing with reusable customers and products
  • Cash-flow visibility with sales and expenses in one place
  • Organized books ready for accountants and tax season
  • Secure access with login, Google sign-in, and role-based permissions

Built with

  • Next.js
  • React
  • NestJS
  • PostgreSQL
  • TypeScript
